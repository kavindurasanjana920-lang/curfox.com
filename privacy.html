Section 01

Who we are and what this policy covers

Curfox is a delivery management platform operated by Parallax Technologies (Pvt) Ltd, a company registered in Sri Lanka (Company Reg. No. PV 00207132), at No. 81/A, High Level Road, Kottawa, Sri Lanka (“Parallax”, “we”, “us”).

This policy explains how we handle personal data when courier companies and other businesses use Curfox, including the staff portal, merchant portal, rider and customer mobile apps, APIs and the curfox.com website.

Our role. Our clients are businesses, usually courier and delivery companies. When they use Curfox to manage deliveries, the client decides what personal data is collected and why, and is the controller of that data. Parallax processes it only on the client’s behalf and on their instructions, as a processor. Parallax is the controller only for the business contact, account and billing information we hold about our own clients, and for data collected through curfox.com.

If you are a delivery recipient or a rider and have a question about your data, please contact the courier company you deal with first. We will help them answer it.

Section 02

What data we process

On behalf of our clients As processor

  • Delivery recipients: name, delivery address, phone number, order and parcel details, cash-on-delivery amounts, delivery status and history, and proof of delivery (photos, signatures, notes).
  • Merchants (the client’s customers): business name, contact details, order data, pickup addresses and settlement information.
  • Riders and drivers: name, contact details, assigned orders, delivery activity and device location while using the rider app for work.
  • Client staff: names, login details and activity in the system.

For our own business As controller

  • Contact, account and billing details of the businesses that use Curfox and their representatives.
  • Support requests and communications with us.
  • Basic technical and usage information from curfox.com.

We do not need, and ask clients not to upload, sensitive personal data such as health, religious or financial account information that is unrelated to deliveries.

Section 03

How we use data

We use client data only to:

  • Provide, operate and support the Curfox platform for that client.
  • Send delivery notifications (SMS, WhatsApp, email) that the client has switched on.
  • Fix problems, keep the system secure and keep it running reliably.
  • Meet our legal obligations.

What we never do:

  • Sell personal data, or share it with anyone for their own marketing.
  • Use one client’s data for another client, or show one client another client’s data.
  • Use delivery recipients’ or riders’ data for our own marketing.

We may use anonymised, aggregated statistics (for example, total order volumes across the platform) to improve Curfox. These cannot identify any person or client.

Section 04

Where data is stored

Hosting. Curfox runs on Huawei Cloud. By default, client data is hosted in Huawei Cloud’s Singapore region. A client can ask for hosting in another region (for example in the GCC) where available.

Separation between clients. Every client’s data is kept in its own separate database, which no other client can access. Larger clients can have their own dedicated cloud environment.

Transfers between countries. Because our clients, our team and our hosting can be in different countries, data may be accessed or stored outside the country where it was collected. Our support and engineering team is based in Sri Lanka. We transfer data only as needed to provide the service, protect it as described in this policy, and follow the cross-border rules of the laws that apply to each client.

Section 05

How we protect data

Security. Data travelling between users and Curfox is encrypted (HTTPS). Customers’ personal details stored in our databases (names, addresses and phone numbers) are encrypted. Access to our servers is limited to authorised Parallax staff with individual accounts, and we keep the platform and its servers updated and monitored.

Secure sign-in. User sessions use short-lived access tokens that expire after 15 minutes by default. Each client’s administrators can change this period to suit their own security needs.

API access. Systems that create orders in Curfox through our API use secure hashed access tokens rather than user logins. A token works only after an authorised staff member of the client has approved it, so no outside system can connect without the client’s permission.

Staff access to client data. Parallax staff do not access a client’s operational data without that client’s explicit approval, for example to investigate a support request the client has raised.

Inside the client’s account. Each client controls which of its own users can see what, through user roles and permissions in Curfox.

Backups. We take full backups every day and can restore a client’s data to any point in the previous 7 days. Backups are kept for up to 2 years and then deleted.

Retention. We keep client data for as long as the client uses Curfox, and handle it at the end of the contract as described in section 7. The client decides how long its own records are kept while it uses the platform.

Section 06

Service providers, incidents and your rights

Service providers. We use a small number of trusted providers to run Curfox: Huawei Cloud for hosting, and messaging providers (SMS, WhatsApp, email) for notifications a client switches on. Payment gateways and other integrations are chosen and contracted by the client. Our providers may only use data to deliver their service to us. A current list is available to clients on request.

Security incidents. If we become aware of a security incident affecting a client’s personal data, we will inform that client without undue delay, and within 72 hours where possible, with the information they need to meet their own legal duties. We will take immediate steps to contain the incident and help the client respond.

Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is used. If Curfox holds your data because you received a delivery or work as a rider, please contact the courier company you deal with; they control your data and we will help them respond. For data Parallax controls, contact us using the details below.

Section 07

Leaving Curfox, our website, and contact

Getting your data back. Clients can download their data in Excel format from Curfox at any time. When a contract ends, we provide a full copy of the client’s data in SQL format within 15 working days of the request, once undisputed fees are settled.

Deletion. After the client confirms it has received its data, we delete it from the live system within 60 days. Copies in backups are deleted as those backups expire, within 2 years at most.

Our website. When you use curfox.com or contact us through it, we collect the details you choose to send (such as your name, company and email) to reply to you. The site uses cookies and similar tools, including advertising pixels, to understand visits and measure our marketing. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Laws we follow. We handle personal data in line with the data protection laws that apply to us and our clients, including Sri Lanka’s Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 (as amended), Oman’s Personal Data Protection Law, the UAE’s Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, and the EU General Data Protection Regulation where it applies.

Changes. We may update this policy as our service or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact. For any question about this policy or your data, write to info@parallax.lk or to Parallax Technologies (Pvt) Ltd, No. 81/A, High Level Road, Kottawa, Sri Lanka.
